Our HTW Berlin paper asks one question: can a moving machine settle a payment on-chain the moment it arrives?

8 s
From delivery trigger to a confirmed Solana transaction. One indoor run, target 15 s.
30+
Outdoor GPS test runs planned to prove it.
3
Nodes in the next design: one fixed drop point and two mobile carriers.
165M
Agent payments on the x402 protocol by April 2026. Machine payments are arriving.

The gap. No prior work combines a moving device with an on-chain payment that arrival triggers. The demo car proves delivery with a QR code. The research track tests GPS arrival.
