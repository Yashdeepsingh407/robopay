A delivery that pays itself.

The money waits in an escrow until the car proves it arrived. No invoice, no click, no bank.

See the full walkthrough

  1. The buyer locks 0.2 SOL

    The money goes into an escrow on Solana. Nobody can touch it yet.

  2. The car delivers and scans the box

    Its camera reads the QR code on the parcel. No code, no payout.

  3. The owner gets paid

    The car signs the delivery on-chain and the escrow releases the money to its owner.
